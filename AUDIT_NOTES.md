# Finance Analytics Platform — Calculation Audit

Corrected: ROE uses average equity; operating ROA uses NOPAT / average assets; ROIC uses NOPAT / average invested capital; FCF uses CFO - absolute CapEx; statement CAGRs sort dates; Beta aligns stock and S&P 500 daily returns; Sortino uses downside deviation relative to MAR; risk-free proxy uses ^IRX with 4% fallback; peer percentage double-scaling was removed.

Platform Score weights: Valuation 25, Growth 20, Quality 20, Financial Health 15, Risk 10, Wall Street 10. Missing components are reweighted rather than treated as zero.

The generic corporate score is intentionally disabled for ETFs/funds, financial institutions, and REITs. Yahoo/yfinance data and analyst consensus can be missing, delayed, restated, or provider-defined. Peer groups remain a curated sector-level starting universe. The Platform Score is a rules-based research aid, not an investment recommendation.
